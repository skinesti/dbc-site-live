Pricing

A starting point for your project

Every project is different, but you shouldn't have to contact me just to find out whether we're even in the same ballpark.

The pricing below reflects the typical starting investment for the kind of work you see throughout my portfolio. Most projects are completed as fixed-price packages, so you'll know the cost before the work begins.

For existing Web sites, smaller updates, troubleshooting, and consulting, hourly work is available at $125/hour.

Web Site Design

Starting at $1,500

Thoughtful, custom Web sites for small businesses that need more than a template and a logo dropped into place.

Whether you're starting from scratch or replacing a site that no longer reflects your business, I'll help shape the structure, messaging, design, and overall experience so everything feels intentional and easy to use.

Web site projects may include:

  • Custom page design
  • Mobile-responsive layouts
  • Content organization and site structure
  • Strategic calls to action
  • Contact or inquiry forms
  • Basic SEO setup
  • Domain and launch support
  • Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, or another platform when appropriate

More involved Web sites, ecommerce, memberships, booking systems, custom integrations, and larger page counts are quoted based on scope.

Custom Software

Starting at $2,500

Sometimes the right solution isn't another off-the-shelf platform.

I design and build custom tools for businesses that need something more specific, from client intake systems and internal dashboards to portals, forms, workflows, calculators, and lightweight SaaS products.

Custom software projects may include:

  • Client or staff portals
  • Intake and onboarding systems
  • Custom forms and workflows
  • Dashboards
  • Scheduling or tracking tools
  • Small business applications
  • Database-driven tools
  • Automations and integrations
  • Custom internal systems
  • Early-stage SaaS products and prototypes

Because software projects can vary dramatically in complexity, each one is scoped and quoted individually.

How pricing works

Most new projects begin with a conversation about what you're trying to accomplish, what already exists, and what actually needs to be built.

From there, I'll recommend the simplest approach that makes sense and provide a clear project scope and price before the work begins.

You won't be pushed into a package full of things you don't need, and you won't have to guess what the project will cost once we're underway.

Have something a little different in mind?

That's often where the most interesting projects start.

If you have a Web site, workflow, client experience, software idea, or digital problem you're trying to solve, tell me what you're working on.
