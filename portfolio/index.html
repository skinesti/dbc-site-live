Portfolio: Websites and software for service-based businesses

I specialize in turning strategic frameworks into functional digital assets, bringing concepts to life through thoughtful construction, technical setup, and operational coordination, so initiatives are executed smoothly from start to finish.

With over 18 years of experience in web development, web design, and graphic design, I create digital experiences that balance structure, usability, and visual clarity. I believe in the power of simplicity and the impact of thoughtful design.

Two brands, and a flagship product of my own

Aqua Pura Spa

A wellness brand and fully custom website developed from the ground up, including logo design, brand identity, and web design. The project involved creating a cohesive, spa-inspired digital experience for a calm, restorative client journey.

Georgia Sapphire Wellness

A new website built around a color palette chosen together with the client, incorporating clean, simple elements alongside their existing logo. The project also included custom aftercare cards for their aesthetic services.

Visit Site →

Clarafide

Clarafide is a branded client intake platform built in-house, currently piloting with Georgia Sapphire Wellness. Clients fill out health history, consent, and consultation forms from their phone, no paper, no clipboard, while real client feedback shapes the product ahead of a wider release.
