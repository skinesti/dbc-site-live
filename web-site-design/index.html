Common questions

How much does a Web site cost?

A one-page Web site is $1,000, four pages is $1,400, six pages is $1,600 and eight pages is $2,000. You pay half to start and half when I turn it over to you. Anything larger is quoted based on scope before the work begins.

Which platform will you build on?

Wix, Squarespace, WordPress or another platform, depending on what suits your business.

Can you update a site I already have?

Yes. Existing sites are supported hourly at $100, or as a fixed project when the scope is larger.

I am a solo esthetician. Is this the right fit?

It is the work I know best. A one-page site with your colors, your booking link and your forms is a strong start for most solo practices, and you can grow into more pages. If you only need booking or consent forms set up, digital solutions is a smaller, lower-cost start, and Clarafide handles treatment forms from $19 a month.
